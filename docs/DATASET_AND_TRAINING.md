# Author-created datasets and prior model training

The study datasets were assembled and prepared by the authors. Captured images were manually annotated using Roboflow. Downloaded datasets already carried labels, which were incorporated into the collections. Dataset creation therefore refers to author preparation of the study collections and does not imply original authorship of every third-party source image.

The object collection contains 11,762 images and ten foreground classes, with original training/validation/test image counts 10,466/866/430. The behavior collection contains 1,149 images and one foreground class, with original counts 804/230/115. Evaluation later uses the documented normalized and exact-overlap-filtered partitions. The behavior export metadata identifies Roboflow version 1, dated 25 April 2025; its metadata declares CC BY 4.0. This export declaration is not independent verification of every upstream image's redistribution rights.

The authors previously fine-tuned the object detector in train3 and the behavior detector in train4 from pretrained yolo11l.pt. Both best.pt checkpoints are used throughout the deployment experiments with their trained weights fixed. No additional training was performed during the manuscript revision. The runs completed 669 and 489 epochs; the selected checkpoints correspond to epochs 469 and 189. Original validation AP50/AP50:95 is 82.40/61.17% for objects and 87.43/53.09% for behavior. These historical validation scores are distinct from the new deployment test scores.

The study/training folder contains the original saved training arguments, complete epoch logs, commands, checkpoint fingerprints and structured summary. These records document actual prior training, not a claim that the historical runs were repeated. Automatic optimizer selection uses SGD; the selected learning rate and momentum shown in the training console were 0.01 and 0.9. Saved argument defaults and checkpoint-internal argument values may differ after automatic optimizer selection; preserve their recorded values rather than silently rewriting them.

The original dataset archives and both trained checkpoints are available through corresponding author Jubayer Al Mahmud (ja.mahmud@just.edu.bd) on reasonable request for research verification, including editorial and peer-review assessment. Raw images and weights remain outside this repository. The authors confirm that the filmed students were over 18 and gave verbal consent for research use and publication. Institutional ethics review was not obtained; no institutional approval or exemption is asserted. The manuscript publishes aggregate figures rather than identifiable participant images.
